  titol: Who we are
  text: 'UTAC is an external service of the Faculty of Psychology of the University of Barcelona. It comprises UTAC Sírius, of the Department of Social Rights and Inclusion, and UTAC Educació, of the Department of Education and Vocational Training of the Government of Catalonia.


    UTAC serves people who can benefit from using augmentative communication, the professionals who support them, and their families. People may use augmentative communication as a result of a motor disability, autism spectrum disorder, intellectual disability, or any other cause that makes it difficult or impossible to use speech to communicate, develop language and/or access learning. UTAC also provides advice on assistive technology in areas such as access to technology, adapted play, environmental control and assisted mobility.'
