  text: '## Augmentative communication and assistive products


    - [AAC Rerc](https://rerc-aac.psu.edu/) - Website of the Rehabilitation Engineering Research Center on Augmentative and Alternative Communication. Resources and information on augmentative communication

    - [AlfaSAAC](https://alfasaac.com/) - Website devoted to outreach and advice on augmentative communication

    - [All Access Life](https://www.allaccesslife.org/) - Website on accessibility with resources organised by type of disability

    - [ARASAAC](https://arasaac.org/) - Aragonese augmentative communication portal. It contains a large collection of pictographic symbols with different levels of abstraction.

    - [Communication Matrix](https://communicationmatrix.org/) - Free tool for assessing communication skills

    - [Eastin](http://www.eastin.eu/es-es/searches/products/index) - European search engine for assistive technology, with ISO 9999 codes

    - [ISAAC](https://www.isaac-online.org/english/webinars/archived-webinars/) - Website of the International Society for Augmentative and Alternative Communication. It has a section devoted entirely to training webinars (some in Spanish)

    - [Low Incidence Resources](https://lowincidenceab.weebly.com/) - Collection of webinars on augmentative communication in English


    ## Software, activities and alternative access


    - [El Buho Boo](http://www.elbuhoboo.com/) - Cause-and-effect games from the keyboard and various activities for practising mouse use through play.

    - [CrisTIC](https://www.cristic.com/1o-2o-y-3o-infantil/informatica/?lang=ca) - Portal with interactive educational activities, including mouse use

    - [Eye Gaze Games](https://www.eyegazegames.com/) - Online games for eye-tracker access

    - [Fungooms](http://www.fungooms.com/) - Simple games and activities for practising with the mouse.

    - [Gazeplay](https://gazeplay.github.io/GazePlay/) - Free collection of games and activities for eye trackers

    - [HelpKidzLearn](http://www.helpkidzlearn.com/) - English-language website with various online activities and games for switch users.

    - [Juegos Atención Temprana](http://chiquitajos.blogspot.com/) - Blog (in Spanish) about interesting games and activities for children in early intervention.

    - [OneSwitch](http://www.oneswitch.org.uk/2/switch-downloads.htm) - Portal devoted to accessible games, with its own blog. You will find many switch games there.

    - [Papunet Games](http://papunet.net/games/) - Website with simple activities and games for practising with a switch or mouse.

    - [PBS Games](https://pbskids.org/games) - Educational games for practising basic mouse skills.

    - [Picto Selector](https://www.pictoselector.eu/es/) - Free software for creating communication boards and books. It includes the ARASAAC symbol library.

    - [Poissonrouge](https://www.poissonrouge.com/) - A large number of activities for practising mouse control through games of varying complexity.

    - [Projecte Fressa](https://projectefressa.blogspot.com/) - Collection of programs for speech synthesis and creating on-screen communication aids, among others, all created by Jordi Lagares.

    - [Schoolsplay](https://sourceforge.net/projects/schoolsplay/) - Includes two programs: Childsplay (for children) and Cognitionplay (for adults), consisting of simple mouse tasks with attention and memory components.

    - [Sebran](https://www.edudiver.com/descargas/sebran.php) and [Minisebran](https://www.edudiver.com/descargas/minisebran.php) - Simple, freely distributed educational programs created by Marianne Wartoft for practising mouse and keyboard use.

    - [Senict Software](https://www.ianbean.co.uk/senict-members-resource-portal/) - Simple cause-and-effect activities and training in very basic scanning, mouse and eye-gaze skills with an eye tracker.

    - [Senswitcher](http://www.senteacher.org/download/10/SEN-Switcher.html) - Simple free cause-and-effect and scanning program. Available to download.

    - [Senteacher](http://www.senteacher.org/) - A selection of educational software.

    - [Tobii Dynavox Accessible Games](https://www.tobiidynavox.com/collections/games) - Simple activities for getting started with a head mouse or eye-gaze mouse.'
