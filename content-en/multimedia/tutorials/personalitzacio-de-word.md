  text: 'To adapt Microsoft Office Word to each student''s needs, there are two main tools: templates and the configuration of the toolbar buttons.


    An interesting and practical option is to use the "Adaptacions Word", which you can find on the [Materials Educatius](/recursos-d-internet/webs-d-interes) website. This application lets you choose from several pre-configured templates designed for different school levels, including options for maths and speech synthesis.


    In some cases, however, it may be worth customising these options individually. The following videos show how to do it.


    If something does not work for you after trying it out, or if you have any questions, you can contact us at [utac@ub.edu](mailto:utac@ub.edu).


    Remember that once a video is playing, you can click on it to watch it in a larger format.'
